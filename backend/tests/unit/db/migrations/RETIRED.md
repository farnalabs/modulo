# Retired migration tests

Date: 2026-11-11

Context: Stage 6 replaced the 244-file historical Alembic chain with four native baseline migrations (`0350_baseline_schema`, `0351_baseline_rls`, `0352_baseline_privileges`, and `0353_baseline_seed_data`). The baseline was verified catalogue-equivalent to the historical chain across 15 schema sections. The tests listed below were retired because they exercised deleted revision modules, downgrade paths, migration-chain positions, intermediate backfills, or source text belonging only to the historical chain. Final-state schema and data coverage remains in the baseline catalogue-equivalence verification and the surviving model/runtime tests; this ledger preserves the audit trail for the historical test corpus rather than weakening assertions until they pass for a different reason.
